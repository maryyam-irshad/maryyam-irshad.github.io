About

Quality is a habit, not a final step

I'm a Software Quality Assurance Engineer with 6.5 years of experience delivering reliable, high-quality software, mostly in healthcare IT.

My QA career started at MTBC, continued at Wiseman Innovations / DataQ Health as a Senior QA Engineer, and today I work at oomi, where I own quality for the company's CRM system and its accounting integrations.

I bring AI into my daily QA work: I use Claude AI in VS Code to analyse requirements, spot gaps and ambiguities early, and draft test cases and scenarios, which I then review, refine and execute. Keeping requirements and test cases in VS Code makes them easy to version, update and reuse. This helps me cover more scenarios in less time without lowering quality.

I work closely with developers and business analysts to clarify requirements early and catch issues before they reach users. I care about data integrity, clear bug reports and releases the team can be confident in.
